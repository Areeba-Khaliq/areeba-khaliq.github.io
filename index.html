Federated Training and Per-Branch Explainability for Hybrid CNN-Transformer Skin Lesion Classification

Independent research project · 2026

Benchmarked four CNN-Transformer architectures for seven-class skin lesion diagnosis on HAM10000, comparing centralized training against federated training across simulated hospitals. Reduced the accuracy gap of federated training through normalization and initialization changes, and built a Grad-CAM++ and attention-rollout pipeline to explain model behaviour across branches.
